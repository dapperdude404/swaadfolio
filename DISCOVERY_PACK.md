## Social preview image — design brief

- Recommended landscape size: **1280 × 640 px** (2:1). Check GitHub's current supported image requirements before uploading.
- Use a clean real product screenshot, legible project name and one accurate sentence about the use case.
- Real recipe catalogue and ingredient guide with accurate photo credits; do not imply future international cuisine has shipped.
- Do not show invented star counts, independent validation, benchmark figures, paid-service permissions or fictional app screens.
- Do not expose API keys, user accounts, private documents, copyrighted photos without permission or personally identifying data.
- GitHub social preview is uploaded via **Settings → General → Social preview** where available. Do not treat this design brief as an uploaded image.
