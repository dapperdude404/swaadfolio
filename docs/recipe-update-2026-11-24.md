# Recipe update — 24 November 2026

Added Jeera Aloo and a savoury, chunky Tomato Chutney, bringing the Indian collection to 14 recipes. Both include English ingredient quantities, Hinglish ingredient names, an ingredient photograph for every row and beginner cooking cues. Potato and lemon photos also fill gaps in existing guides.

These are simplified home-style versions written for Swaadfolio, not claims of a single definitive regional recipe. Preparation times include the described preparation and cooking; actual times vary with equipment and ingredient size. The recipes have not been kitchen-tested by this project.

## Research references

- [Dassana Amit: Jeera Aloo](https://www.vegrecipesofindia.com/jeera-aloo-recipe-jeera-aloo/) — boiled potato and cumin method.
- [Manjula Jain: Tomato Chutney](https://manjulaskitchen.com/tomato-chutney/) — cooked tomato, ginger and cumin approach; our version omits sugar, cornstarch and several spices.

Recipe and ingredient photographs, authors and licences are listed in `dist/credits.html`. Pictures show representative ingredients and dishes rather than photographs of a kitchen-tested batch of these precise recipes.
